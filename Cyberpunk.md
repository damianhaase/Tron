# Cyberpunk

In cyberpunk, information is the primary weapon, resource, and battlefield, blurring the lines between digital and physical conflict. Instead of traditional armies clashing with conventional weapons, information warfare is waged through clandestine hacking, propaganda, and economic manipulation, typically by powerful corporations or state actors. This "high tech, low life" conflict comments on how ubiquitous technology can be used to control and oppress the masses.

## The battlefield of information

**Cyberspace as a battleground:** Direct, physical warfare is often replaced or supplemented by digital conflict that can be just as destructive. High-stakes "netrunning" sees elite hackers fighting in cyberspace to steal secrets, sabotage infrastructure, and manipulate markets. Examples of this include the "DataKrash" in the Cyberpunk lore, and the destructive digital conflicts of the Fourth Corporate War.

**Corporate dominance:** In the vacuum of failed governments, mega-corporations rise to become the dominant world powers. They use information warfare as their primary tool to gain a competitive advantage, conducting industrial espionage and using digital attacks to cause economic disruption for rivals.

**Targeting critical infrastructure:** Attacks are often directed at civilian technologies that control utilities, financial systems, and communication networks. These targets are more vulnerable to cyberattacks, as controlling physical space is more difficult than controlling digital infrastructure.

## The weaponization of information

**Manipulation and disinformation:** Information itself is weaponized as propaganda to control the population and manage public perception. Corporate news agencies and online networks are used to spread misinformation and influence the choices of adversaries.

**Erosion of privacy:** The mass-integration of technology into daily life and the ubiquitous surveillance it enables leads to a near-total loss of privacy. This data is collected and used by corporations and governments for social control, making it difficult for citizens to defend against manipulation and exploitation.

**Economic disruption:** Cyberattacks on financial channels, stock exchanges, and payment systems can cause massive economic damage, disrupting economies and causing financial losses.

**Identity theft and erasure:** Digital identity is a core concept, and manipulating or erasing it is a powerful form of attack. Cyberpunk stories show how people's digital records can be altered, stolen, or destroyed to erase their existence from databases.

## The soldier of information

**Elite hackers and netrunners:** "Netrunners" or elite hackers operate in cyberspace, using specialized interfaces to breach secure corporate networks. They are the frontline soldiers in information warfare, performing clandestine operations to steal data or commit sabotage.

**Blurred accountability:** In information warfare, it can be nearly impossible to trace the origin of an attack, complicating accountability. For automated or cyberattacks, determining who is responsible for a particular event can be a difficult challenge.

**The individual's struggle:** Information warfare is primarily a struggle for power waged by vast, impersonal forces. This leaves the individual to navigate a high-tech, dystopian world where truth is subjective and privacy is non-existent, and where the only way to survive is to be resourceful and cunning.
