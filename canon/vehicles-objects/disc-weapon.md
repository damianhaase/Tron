# Disc Weapon

## Appearance
- Flat, circular disc made of glowing neon light
- Recognizable as [Tron](../characters/tron.md)'s primary weapon
- Can be thrown and recalled at will
- Glows with neon intensity; visible in flight
- Appears to return to thrower like a boomerang (or is retrieved)

## Mechanics
- Weapon of choice for program combat
- Must be thrown with precision and timing
- Can be caught/deflected by other programs
- Lethal when strikes a program (causes deresolution/deletion)
- Requires skill and accuracy; not a spray weapon

## Function in Tron (1982)
- Primary weapon for [Tron](../characters/tron.md) and combat programs
- Symbol of [Tron](../characters/tron.md)'s precision and justice
- Used in combat against [Sark](../characters/sark.md) and MCP enforcers
- Final tool: [Tron](../characters/tron.md) uses disc to attack [The MCP](../characters/the-mcp.md) core

## Key Moments
- **Disc vs. Disc combat:** Programs battle with surgical precision
- **Tron's mastery:** [Tron](../characters/tron.md)'s control and accuracy demonstrate his skill
- **Attack on MCP core:** Disc strikes at system's vulnerable geometric structure

## Thematic Significance
- **Precision over power:** Disc requires skill, not raw force
- **Justice as precise:** [Tron](../characters/tron.md)'s disc as instrument of measured justice
- **Program combat aesthetic:** Geometric, ballistic, clean
- **In [Sark](../characters/sark.md)'s hands:** Violence without purpose; enforcement without justice

## Symbolic Function
- Represents program agency and self-defense
- In [Tron](../characters/tron.md)'s hands: precision, justice, moral clarity
- In [Sark](../characters/sark.md)'s hands: violence and enforcement of unjust authority
- The weapon follows its wielder's moral character
