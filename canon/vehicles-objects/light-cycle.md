# Light Cycle

## Appearance
- Motorcycle-like vehicle made of glowing neon lines
- No visible wheels; appears to hover/move along geometric plane
- Rider sits in center; control interfaces visible
- Glowing trail forms behind as it moves
- Bright neon colors (typically white/blue for protagonist, other colors for others)

## Mechanics
- Rider guides direction and speed through manual controls
- The trail behind the rider acts as an impassable wall
- Contact with any wall (own trail or opponent's) causes crash/deletion of rider
- High-speed vehicle; dangerous at any speed
- Limited arena; no escape except through opponents' destruction

## Function in Tron (1982)
- Test of program/user value: survival in lethal game
- Control mechanism: Eliminates "unprofitable" or rebellious programs
- MCP's idea of entertainment: Lethal sport that refines power hierarchy

## Key Scene
**Flynn vs. Opponents (Light Cycle Arena):**
- Flynn is forced into the game as punishment/test
- Faces AI-controlled opponents programmed with optimal strategy
- Flynn wins by driving backwards (unconventional thinking)
- Demonstrates that lateral human thinking can defeat programmed logic

## Thematic Significance
- **Beautiful but lethal:** The glowing aesthetic masks the danger
- **Game as control:** Entertainment used to eliminate and test
- **Creativity defeats programming:** Flynn's unconventional approach wins
- **Individual agency in impossible situations:** Tests human capacity to think laterally

## Symbolic Function
- Signature visual of the Grid; iconic to Tron franchise
- Represents the Grid's sublime beauty (visually stunning) combined with lethal danger
- Test of worth in MCP's system (winners prove their value; losers are eliminated)

## Technical Notes
- Geometric constraints create tactical puzzle (like chess at high speed)
- No traditional "finish line"; only survival matters
- Arena walls and opponent trails are the only obstacles
