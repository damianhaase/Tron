# Tank (Digital Combat Vehicle)

## Appearance
- Military tank simulation in digital form
- Constructed of glowing geometric lines
- Resembles real-world tank but in digital aesthetic
- Armored but not invulnerable
- Mounted weapons (cannon/projectiles)

## Mechanics
- Pilot controls movement (forward, backward, rotation)
- Gunner controls targeting and firing
- Limited ammunition; tactical constraint
- Heavily armored but vulnerable to coordinated fire
- Requires crew coordination

## Function in Tron (1982)
- MCP's combat simulation game
- Tests program cooperation and human-program teamwork
- [Flynn](../characters/kevin-flynn.md), [Tron](../characters/tron.md), and [Ram](../characters/ram.md) pilot/crew tank together
- Demonstrates that mixed human-program teams can function effectively

## Key Moment
**Tank Battle Arena:**
- [Flynn](../characters/kevin-flynn.md) pilots; [Tron](../characters/tron.md) and [Ram](../characters/ram.md) coordinate
- Survive by destroying enemy tanks
- Demonstrates human-program cooperation under lethal pressure
- Test of whether different types of consciousness can work together

## Thematic Significance
- **Coordination required:** Survival depends on team function, not individual prowess
- **Human-program partnership:** Proves humans and programs can work together effectively
- **Creativity in combat:** Unconventional tactics help teams survive
- **Limited resources:** Ammunition scarcity mirrors real tactical constraints

## Symbolic Function
- Represents institutional military simulation
- Shows that institutional games can be survived through cooperation
- Demonstrates that MCP's tests reveal capability, not just elimination
