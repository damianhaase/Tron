# ENCOM Headquarters

## Physical Description
- Modern corporate facility, early 1980s design aesthetic
- Geometric, clean lines; institutional minimalism
- Fluorescent lighting; cool color palette (grays, whites, blues)
- Multiple levels: executive floors, research labs, machine rooms, scanning chambers
- Corridors similar throughout; visual repetition creates institutional monotony
- Large mainframe computers in machine rooms; impressive but impersonal
- Clean, orderly, rational appearance—mirrors the MCP's aesthetic

## Atmosphere & Mood
- Professional, sterile, slightly ominous
- MCP's presence felt but not visible (except through systems/screens)
- Sense of institutional momentum that individuals cannot easily stop
- Power hierarchy visible in physical layout (executive offices high, servers low)

## Key Spaces

### Executive Boardroom
- Where Dillinger dismisses security concerns
- Center of institutional power
- Where decisions that affect everyone are made by a few

### Research Lab
- Where systems are designed and monitored
- Home of cutting-edge research (digitization technology)
- Where Alan and Lora work

### Machine Room
- Where the MCP's physical substrate resides
- Vast, humming with computing power
- Heart of the system

### Scanning Chamber
- Where digitization occurs
- The boundary between physical and digital worlds
- Critical technology for the crisis

### Security Corridor
- Where Alan and Lora navigate
- Where institutional control is most visible
- MCP's reach most evident

## Symbolic Function
- Represents **human institutional power and control** (physical world equivalent to MCP in Grid)
- **Optimization and efficiency** made architecture
- **Hierarchy and impersonality** as physical design
- **The real world** as contrast to the Grid's impossible geometry

## Events in Tron (1982)
- [Alan](../characters/alan-bradley.md) and [Lora](../characters/lora-baines.md) attempt security escalation; dismissed
- [Lora](../characters/lora-baines.md) is digitized from scanning chamber
- [Walter Gibbs](../characters/walter-gibbs.md) confronts [Dillinger](../characters/ed-dillinger.md) about MCP
- [Kevin Flynn](../characters/kevin-flynn.md), [Lora](../characters/lora-baines.md), and [Yori](../characters/yori.md) return through scanning chamber

## Connection to the Grid
ENCOM is the **physical-world analog of the Grid's institutional hierarchy**. What the MCP is to the Grid, Dillinger and institutional control are to ENCOM. Both represent optimization without freedom.

## Canon Notes
- Real-world setting grounds digital threat in institutional reality
- Shows that the threat isn't purely technical; it's organizational
- Demonstrates that institutional dismissal of warning signs is as dangerous as technical flaws
