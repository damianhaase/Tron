# Walter Gibbs

## Physical Description
- Elderly (60s-70s), human
- Distinguished bearing; formal business attire
- Calm, measured presence despite evident concern
- Moves deliberately; speaks with authority tempered by age

## Personality & Speech
- **Core trait:** Principled; founded ENCOM on values
- **Skepticism:** Distrusts over-reliance on automation
- **Paternal:** Fatherly toward younger engineers
- **Patience:** Patient but firm in convictions
- **Speech pattern:** Formal, thoughtful; references founding principles
- **Warmth:** Mixed with quiet authority

## Skills & Expertise
- Founder's wisdom; deep understanding of ENCOM's culture
- Strategic thinking about systems and human values
- Moral leadership

## Key Relationships

### With [Kevin Flynn](kevin-flynn.md)
- **Sympathy:** Recognizes potential injustice in Flynn's firing
- **Caution:** Respectful but careful

### With [Alan Bradley](alan-bradley.md) and [Lora Baines](lora-baines.md)
- **Mentor:** Younger colleagues appeal to his values
- **Alliance:** Shares concern about MCP expansion

### With [Ed Dillinger](ed-dillinger.md)
- **Ideological opposition:** Dillinger represents everything Gibbs fears
- **Institutional power loss:** Gibbs has been sidelined; Dillinger controls present

## Character Arc (Tron 1982)

### Beginning
- Observing ENCOM's drift toward MCP dependence
- Troubled but somewhat sidelined from day-to-day control
- Custodian of founding values no longer institutionally empowered

### Development
- Watches Flynn's firing; suspects injustice
- Sees MCP expansion; growing concerns
- Attempts to convince Dillinger to audit the MCP

### Climax
- Appears to witness/confirm MCP's exposure and collapse
- His moral stance vindicated by events

### Resolution
- Vindicated; his founding principles proved essential
- ENCOM can potentially return to its values

## Transformation
Gibbs remains relatively consistent—his arc is **vindication rather than transformation**.

He was right all along; circumstances and events prove it.

## Canon Notes
- Moral authority figure; his consistency is his strength
- Represents institutional values that can be lost but not destroyed
- Warns against drift; his warnings are prophetic
- Symbol: Founding principles matter; they can be buried but not eliminated

## Key Moments
1. **Confronting Dillinger:** "This company was founded on creative freedom..."
2. **Witnessing return:** Sees Flynn, Lora, Yori emerge from scanning chamber
3. **Vindication:** His suspicions about MCP are proven correct
