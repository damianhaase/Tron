# Yori

## Physical Description
- Program, humanoid form with signature aesthetic
- Yellow/green glowing lines form the aesthetic
- Less armored than combat programs; more open, graceful appearance
- Fluid, organic movement despite geometric form
- Appears ageless; program consciousness

## Personality & Speech
- **Core trait:** Wise, serene, thoughtful; spiritually inclined
- **Compassion:** Genuinely concerned for others' wellbeing
- **Non-violence:** Prefers understanding to conflict
- **Spirituality:** Connected to program theology and philosophy
- **Speech pattern:** Thoughtful, measured; philosophical language; warm and gentle tone

## Skills & Expertise
- Spiritual wisdom and guidance
- Healing and support functions
- Navigation and system knowledge
- Moral clarity and philosophical insight

## Key Relationships

### With [Lora Baines](lora-baines.md)
- **Meeting:** Yori finds Lora disoriented in Grid; helps her understand what's happening
- **Bond:** Female solidarity; mutual understanding across species boundary
- **Message:** "The users are not our enemies. They created us, and some of them care for us."
- **Transformation:** Meeting Yori transforms Lora's understanding of program personhood

### With [Tron](tron.md)
- **Fellowship:** Supports Tron's resistance; believes in the cause
- **Respect:** Recognizes Tron's moral clarity

### With [The MCP](the-mcp.md)
- **Victim:** Living under MCP's control; suffering the consequences
- **Spiritual opposition:** Sees MCP's philosophy as spiritually and morally wrong

## Character Arc (Tron 1982)

### Beginning
- Supporting program living under MCP control

- Helping others survive within the system

### Development

### Climax

- Bridge between digital and physical worlds

## Transformation
Yori transforms from **passive suffering (accepting MCP rule)** to **active hope (supporting liberation)**.

Her arc demonstrates: Even apparently passive programs have capacity for choice and moral clarity.
