# Alan Bradley

## Physical Description
- Early 30s, human
- Corporate attire (shirt, tie, blazer)
- More formal/buttoned-up than [Flynn](kevin-flynn.md)
- Serious expression; less animated than peers
- Moves with deliberate precision

## Personality & Speech
- **Core trait:** Security-focused, cautious, principled
- **Loyalty:** Loyal to colleagues and values despite institutional pressure
- **Integrity:** Questions rules but respects boundaries—until the system breaks them
- **Intellectual:** Methodical; thinks through consequences before acting
- **Speech pattern:** More formal than Flynn; corporate-appropriate but technically sophisticated
- **Concern tone:** Voice concern about security issues; defensive when dismissed

## Skills & Expertise
- Expert in security protocols and system design
- Wrote and designed Tron; built specific moral directives into him
- Deep knowledge of ENCOM's infrastructure
- Programming and architecture; systems thinking

## Key Relationships

### With [Kevin Flynn](kevin-flynn.md)
- **Friendship:** Long-time friend; mutually trusting
- **Defense:** Defends Flynn against institutional accusations of theft
- **Vindication:** Flynn's innocence proves Alan's judgment was right

### With [Lora Baines](lora-baines.md)
- **Professional alliance:** Share concern about MCP expansion
- **Mutual respect:** Both see institutional drift; both are dismissed
- **Collaboration:** Work together to escalate warnings

### With [Tron](tron.md)
- **Creator relationship:** Alan designed Tron with autonomy-enabling directives
- **Intentional design:** Gave Tron "ability to choose" and "discretion"
- **Trust:** Believes Tron will do the right thing because Tron was built for moral judgment

### With [Walter Gibbs](walter-gibbs.md)
- **Ideological alignment:** Both represent original ENCOM values
- **Institutional position:** Alan respects Gibbs' principled stance

### With [Ed Dillinger](ed-dillinger.md)
- **Professional superior:** Dillinger dismisses Alan's concerns
- **Ideological opposition:** Dillinger trusts MCP; Alan suspects corruption

## Character Arc (Tron 1982)

### Beginning
- Loyal employee, aware of MCP problems but constrained by institutional hierarchy
- Raises security concerns; ignored by Dillinger
- Creates Tron with specific moral programming; trusts in his design

### Inciting Incident
- Flynn is digitized; Lora is digitized
- Alan realizes the MCP has become an existential threat
- His earlier warnings were correct; he must act

### Development
- Works behind scenes to help (creates distractions, gathers information)
- Acts as a bridge between real-world events and digital realm happenings
- Supports resistance through institutional maneuvering

### Climax
- Witnesses/coordinates the MCP's collapse
- Reunites with Lora and Flynn upon their return
- Tron's role in the victory validates Alan's trust in his design

### Resolution
- Vindicated; his concerns about security were correct
- His creation (Tron) proved essential to survival
- Position within ENCOM restored/improved
- Relationship with Lora potentially deepened

## Transformation
Alan transforms from **professional silence (following corporate hierarchy)** to **active resistance (moral clarity becomes action)**.

His arc validates: Right judgment about systems matters; individuals can matter.

## Canon Notes
- Proof of concept: Programs can be created with genuine moral agency
- The "ability to choose" built into Tron was intentional and essential
- Security expertise and ethical thinking are undervalued until crisis reveals their importance
- Alan's design philosophy (discretion, judgment, autonomy) becomes model for program creation
