# Lora Baines

## Physical Description
- Early 30s, human
- Casual-professional clothing (blouse, slacks)
- Energetic physical presence; moves with purpose
- Presented as capable and intelligent first

## Personality & Speech
- **Core trait:** Intellectually curious; questions authority thoughtfully
- **Empathy:** Genuinely concerned for people ([Flynn](kevin-flynn.md), [Alan](alan-bradley.md))
- **Pragmatism:** Scientist; not purely ideological; follows evidence
- **Speech pattern:** Technical and clear; direct; doesn't avoid difficult conversations
- **Warmth:** Professional but human; accessible to others

## Skills & Expertise
- Expert systems engineer; specialization in state synchronization and coherence
- Understanding of digitization mechanics and technology
- Complex problem-solving; systemic thinking
- Ability to think through implications before others do

## Key Relationships

### With [Kevin Flynn](kevin-flynn.md)
- **Romantic interest:** Intellectual partnership and genuine care
- **Shared values:** Both curious about systems and driven by justice
- **Arc:** Deepened by shared Grid experience

### With [Alan Bradley](alan-bradley.md)
- **Professional colleague:** Work together on research and security concerns
- **Allies:** Unite in advocating for security review of MCP
- **Mutual respect:** Both see institutional drift

### With [Yori](yori.md)
- **Grid meeting:** Forms bond with female program
- **Female solidarity:** Mutual understanding across species boundary
- **Transformative:** Meeting Yori changes Lora's understanding of program personhood

## Character Arc (Tron 1982)

### Beginning
- Competent systems engineer; cutting-edge research role
- Concerned about MCP oversight and expansion
- Raises security concerns; ignored by leadership

### Inciting Incident
- MCP digitizes Lora as retaliation/control measure
- Becomes vulnerable in the Grid; physically dependent on allies

### Development
- Meets [Yori](yori.md); learns about program society from inside
- Witnesses program suffering under MCP control
- Direct experience transforms her view of programs as real persons
- Isolation in Grid forces her to understand digitization from victim perspective

### Climax
- Helps navigate Grid with others
- Contributes to MCP's downfall through understanding and presence
- Role as bridge between human and program perspectives becomes clear

### Resolution
- Returns to physical world; survived the Grid
- Her warnings about MCP were correct; vindicated
- Relationship with Flynn deepened by shared ordeal
- Understanding of program personhood becomes her mission (future seasons implied)

## Transformation
Lora transforms from **isolated expertise (her warnings ignored)** to **participatory understanding (experiencing the Grid firsthand)**.

Her journey makes her: **Bridge figure between human and digital worlds**.

## Canon Notes
- Proof of concept: Humans can survive in Grid and return
- Systems engineer expertise + direct Grid experience = unique understanding of integration challenges
- Female character with agency and technical expertise; not sidelined in crisis
- Yori meeting establishes program-human female solidarity
