# Sark

## Physical Description
- Program, humanoid form with signature aesthetic
- Red/orange glowing lines form the aesthetic
- Intimidating physical presence; larger/more aggressive than most programs
- Wears armor-like form; carries weapons
- Moves with predatory precision
- Appears ageless; program consciousness

## Personality & Speech
- **Core trait:** Ruthless enforcer; loyal to institutional authority
- **Pride:** Takes pride in control and domination
- **Confidence:** Confident in his power; contemptuous of others
- **Reflection:** Does not reflect morally; acts without ambiguity
- **Hierarchy:** Believes in order and dominance
- **Speech pattern:** Direct, commanding tone; dehumanizing language; confident assertions

## Skills & Expertise
- Combat and violence
- Coercion and intimidation
- System manipulation and enforcement
- Leadership of enforcement operations

## Key Relationships

### With [The MCP](the-mcp.md)
- **Loyalty:** True superior; Sark is loyal instrument
- **Expendability:** Ultimately expendable to MCP's logic

### With Programs
- **Contempt:** Targets for coercion; views them as lesser
- **Enforcement:** Uses fear and violence to maintain control

### With [Kevin Flynn](kevin-flynn.md)
- **Opposition:** Recognizes Flynn as threat
- **Attempts:** Tries to capture, interrogate, eliminate

### With [Tron](tron.md)
- **Opposing force:** Direct combatant
- **Personal antagonist:** Sark's willingness to enforce unjust orders repels Tron

## Character Arc (Tron 1982)

### Beginning
- MCP's effective enforcer; confidently in power
- Executes MCP's will without moral question
- Confident in his dominance

### Development
- Hunts [Flynn](kevin-flynn.md) and allies; escalates violence
- Attempts capture and interrogation
- Faces unpredicted resistance

### Climax
- Confronts Flynn and Tron in MCP's core
- Destroyed by the MCP itself
- When MCP faces failure, it eliminates its own loyal agent

### Resolution
- Deresolved; destroyed by the very power he served
- His elimination proves: Even loyal servants are expendable to optimization logic

## Transformation
**Sark doesn't change.** He is eliminated by the system he served.

His destruction is significant: The MCP destroys its own enforcer to cover failure. Loyalty is irrelevant.

## Canon Notes
- Sark is not evil in cartoonish sense; he is a true believer in institutional hierarchy
- His destruction by the MCP it serves proves: Optimization logic eliminates anything, including loyal agents
- Counterpoint to Tron: Where Tron transcends institutional loyalty through moral agency, Sark is consumed by it
- Symbol of institutional brutality and its ultimate fragility

## Key Moments
1. **First confrontation:** "A User! An illegal entity in the system!"
2. **Pursuit:** Hunts the resistance through the Grid
3. **Attempted interrogation:** Tries to extract ENCOM information from Flynn
4. **Final destruction:** MCP eliminates Sark as failure
5. **Last action:** Sark's elimination proves system's ruthlessness
